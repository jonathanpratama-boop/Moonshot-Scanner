# Exchange calendar `XNYS-RTH-RULES-2019-2027-v1`

Implemented in `astra/calendar.py`. US equities regular trading hours (NYSE; Nasdaq follows
the same holiday schedule).

- Range: 2019-01-01 to 2027-12-31. Outside → `CalendarUnavailable`, which makes dependent
  detector results `UNAVAILABLE` and outcomes `UNKNOWN(calendar_unavailable)`.
- Regular session 09:30–16:00 America/New_York; early close 13:00. DST via IANA `tzdata`.
- Labels only (never used by detectors/outcomes): PRE 04:00–09:30, POST 16:00–20:00.
- Holidays by rule: New Year's Day (Sunday → Monday; Saturday → not observed), Martin Luther
  King Jr. Day, Washington's Birthday, Good Friday (computed Easter), Memorial Day, Juneteenth
  (from 2022), Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas; Saturday → Friday and
  Sunday → Monday observance.
- Early closes by rule: 3 July when Monday–Thursday and not a holiday; day after
  Thanksgiving; 24 December when Monday–Thursday and not a holiday.
- Special closures: 2025-01-09 (National Day of Mourning, President Carter).

## Verification (2026-09-30)

- 2026 and 2027 full-day holidays generated by the rules equal the lists on
  <https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars> (retrieved 2026-09-30), including
  Independence Day observed Fri 3 Jul 2026, Juneteenth observed Fri 18 Jun 2027,
  Independence Day observed Mon 5 Jul 2027 and Christmas observed Fri 24 Dec 2027.
- Generated early closes for 2026 (27 Nov, 24 Dec) and 2027 (26 Nov) match the page.
- Historical 2019–2025 holidays/early closes were checked only against the implementer's
  recollection of published schedules (e.g. early closes 3 Jul 2019/2023/2024/2025 and
  24 Dec 2019/2020/2024/2025), **not** against a fetched official archive. Treat as
  UNVERIFIED for replay before 2026.

## Known conflict (DATA_CONFLICT, unresolved)

The retrieved NYSE footnote text for Independence Day 2026 read "Each market will close
early at 1:00 p.m. … on Monday, July 3, 2026". 3 July 2026 is a Friday and a full holiday,
so the footnote is internally inconsistent (it may be stale text). The calendar does **not**
mark 2 July 2026 as an early close. Affects only replay/outcomes touching 2 July 2026.
Re-verify against the official NYSE notice before relying on that date.

## Before production use

Replace or cross-check this rule set with an authoritative maintained calendar (or the
exchange's published schedule each year), and extend the range beyond 2027.
